DTRV — three UI directions

Same features in each: 4 toggles, leave/day-off/others, 3-digit code that records on the 3rd digit, a notification you can tap to change the time, and My DTR with the late count. Each option shows the Record screen with the late notification and the My DTR screen. Pick one, or mix parts (e.g. A's record screen + B's calendar).

A

Clarity — light and calm

Light app look, like banking apps. Toggles have icons and labels, the code shows as 3 boxes so people see it's exactly 3 digits, and the notification works like a phone alert. Best for: mixed ages, bright offices, the safest choice.

Night Shift — dark, dialer style

Dark screen with a big clock, the 4 slots as one segmented bar, and a round keypad like the phone dialer that people already know. The notification shows lateness as a bar. My DTR is a calendar heat-map: green on time, orange late, purple leave. Best for: phone use, low light, a modern look; the calendar shows patterns of lates at a glance.

Sunrise — big coloured tiles

Warm and friendly, with each slot its own colour (orange morning, yellow lunch, teal afternoon, indigo going home). After a few days people tap by colour without reading. The confirmation is a bottom sheet with the time very large, sitting where the thumb is. Best for: shared kiosk PCs or tablets, older staff, fewest mis-taps.
