How it works

  1. A tile is pre-selected by the time of day. Tap another tile if needed.
  2. Type your 3-digit code (e.g. 024). It records on the 3rd digit.
  3. A sheet slides up with the time in large digits, and a Late warning plus your late count this month. Flexi employees also see their time out.
  4. Change time to match the biometric, Undo if you mistyped, or Done. It closes by itself after 8 s.
  5. Leave · Day-off · Holiday · Others: tap one, type your code, pick the dates.
  6. My DTR: tap it, type your code. You'll see your month, lates, edits and Print.
📱Safe spacing: content starts below the notch/status bar and ends above the home bar. On a real phone it uses the phone's own safe-area values, so nothing hides under the notch or the gesture bar.
