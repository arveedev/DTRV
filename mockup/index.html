How it works (Rail · dark)

  1. The 4 tiles in a row show today's times for the last code used on this phone (your phone remembers you). A tile is pre-selected by time of day; tap another if needed.
  2. Type your 3-digit code on the round keypad. It records on the 3rd digit.
  3. A sheet slides up: the time in large digits, Late by N min · Nth late this month, today's progress (1 of 4), and the flexi time out.
  4. Change time to match the biometric, Undo a mistyped code, or Done. It closes by itself after 5 s.
  5. Leave · Day-off · Holiday · Others: tap one, type your code, pick the dates.
  6. My DTR: late count, a colour calendar, and tap a day to see or edit it. Print without the admin PIN.
📱Safe spacing: content starts below the notch and ends above the home bar. Smaller phones get smaller keys so nothing is ever cut off.
