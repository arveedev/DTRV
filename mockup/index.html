How the employee screen works

  1. The app pre-selects a toggle from the time of day. Tap another toggle if needed.
  2. Type your 3-digit code (2-digit employee numbers start with 0, e.g. 024). It records on the 3rd digit, no button needed.
  3. A notification drops down with your name, the time, and a Late badge plus how many lates this month. Flexi employees also see their expected out time.
  4. Tap the notification to change the time, e.g. to match the office biometric.
  5. For a day off, tap On leave / Day-off / Others, type your code, then pick the dates.
  6. My DTR shows your month, late count, and lets you edit any day or print.

Change the Demo clock above to test, e.g. set 8:20 AM and type 024.
